How we calculated this

The estimate is built from four value levers that a Virtual Captive™ program captures and other approaches typically don't. Each is measured as an annual dollar gap versus a full captive baseline:

Finance income
Interest-rate spread on financed sales that flows to a bank or goes uncaptured today, instead of being retained through your own program.
Lost sales
Deals that fall through — or go to a competing brand — because financing wasn't fast, flexible, or available enough at the point of sale.
Retention
Repeat purchases lost at trade-in or replacement time because no one owns the customer's payment relationship — valued across a customer's next several replacement cycles, not just this year's sale.
Brand impact
Customers who do get financed, but through an experience — slow, opaque, or misaligned with their interests — bad enough to cost you their next purchase. Highest where a single outside lender runs point-of-sale financing across your whole dealer network.

The retention and brand-impact levers value a lost customer across their next few replacement cycles (not just one missed sale), using your category's typical replacement cycle and an 8% annual discount rate applied over 3 future cycles — the further out a repeat purchase sits, the less it's worth today.
